# Ingenuity flight 26 and the parachute/backshell field

- `flight26_colour_frames.png`: the 10 colour frames (HELI_RTE) of 2022-04-20 (Perseverance sol 414): backshell, parachute and debris, plus a line of rocks.
- `flight26_nav_frames_every7th.png`: every 7th of the 190 downward navigation frames (HELI_NAV). The backshell is the bright patch at left in frames t = 115-127 s.
- `debris_field_ESP_068294.png`: the same objects in HiRISE ESP_068294 (80 m window, 25 cm pixels shown 4x): the large crumpled patch is the parachute, the compact bright spot at lower left is the backshell.
- `new_objects_vs_2016.json`: the 25 strongest new bright objects between the 2016 and the 2021 pass; the cluster near (-1.1 km E, +0.2 km N of the landing spot) is the parachute and backshell.
Downloads and the search code are in `scripts/ingenuity/`. The raw frames are not stored here (NASA raw-image API, category `ingenuity`, sol 414).
